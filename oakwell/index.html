About Oakwell

Memory care in a quiet San Antonio neighborhood, with a private room for every resident and secure indoor and outdoor spaces.

Nestled in a beautiful and quiet neighborhood, our beautifully updated community offers a caring and supportive environment for our residents.

At Gemstone at Oakwell, we specialize in personalized care, including dedicated memory care services, to meet the unique needs of each person. Residents enjoy nutritious meals, engaging activities and a lifestyle designed to support their well-being. Our intimate community has private rooms for all residents, for comfort and privacy.

Surrounded by lush greenery, our campus has secure indoor and outdoor spaces for relaxation and recreation. With all-inclusive services, you can have peace of mind knowing your loved one is getting the dedicated care they deserve.

Questions families ask

Have a question that is not here? Call (210) 826-6594.

What healthcare resources are available here?

Gemstone at Oakwell offers primary care providers, podiatry, dental, physical therapy and hospice services, all provided in house.

What activities does the community provide?

Residents pick the activities at the monthly resident council meeting, and volunteers give their time to support the community too.

Can residents leave the community at any time?

Yes. This is your loved one's home. The only requirement is that you sign your loved one out and request their daily medications if needed.
