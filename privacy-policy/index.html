Privacy policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026

Gemstone Senior Living Management LLC ("Gemstone," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website gemstoneslc.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

When you fill out our Contact Us form, we collect the following information, all of which is required to submit the form:

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Message

We do not currently sell products or services directly through the Site (no e-commerce), and we do not collect payment information through the Site.

Our Site is hosted on the Wix platform, which may automatically collect certain technical information (such as browser type, device information, and cookies) to operate and secure the Site. Please see Section 6 below for more on cookies.

2. How we use your information

We use the information collected through the Contact Us form to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request;
  • Communicate with you about our senior living services;
  • Contact you by phone, email, or text message (SMS) if you have provided consent, as described below; and
  • Improve our Site and the services we offer.

3. SMS / text messaging communications

If you provide your phone number through our Contact Us form, you consent to receive text messages (SMS) from Gemstone related to your inquiry and our services.

Your phone number and your SMS consent will never be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. This information is used solely by Gemstone to communicate with you.

  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Gemstone.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the information you provide (name, email, message) with:

  • Employees or contractors of Gemstone who need it to respond to your inquiry;
  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (such as our website host, Wix), under confidentiality obligations; and
  • Third parties if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental request.

Exception for phone numbers and SMS consent: Regardless of any other provision in this Policy, your phone number and your consent to receive SMS messages will not be shared with, or sold to, any third party for any purpose, including marketing.

5. Data retention

We retain the information you submit through the Contact Us form for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain appropriate business records, or as required by law.

6. Cookies and website hosting

Our Site is built and hosted on Wix. Wix may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember preferences, and gather aggregate usage statistics. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect how the Site functions.

7. Your choices and rights

You may:

  • Reply STOP to any text message to opt out of future SMS communications;
  • Contact us at admin@gemstoneslc.com to request that we correct, update, or delete the information you have provided; and
  • Contact us with any questions about how your information is used.

8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Effective Date," and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is posted on the Site.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at:

Gemstone Senior Living Management LLC
Email: admin@gemstoneslc.com
Website: gemstoneslc.com
