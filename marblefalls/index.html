About Marble Falls

Studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments near the Highland Lakes, with chef-prepared meals every day.

Our community provides a warm and supportive environment where residents feel at home. Through personalized care, modern amenities and engaging daily activities, we help residents enjoy a comfortable and fulfilling lifestyle, surrounded by the beauty of the Highland Lakes.

Our studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments are designed to support independence while providing the safety and peace of mind residents and families value. Spacious layouts, comfortable living areas and senior-friendly features let residents enjoy their own space while staying connected to the community.

Care and services

Residents enjoy personalized care, daily chef-prepared meals, engaging activities and supportive services such as housekeeping, laundry and help with daily living, with attentive staff in a peaceful setting near the Highland Lakes.

  • Personal care

    Compassionate help with bathing, dressing and medication management, so residents keep their dignity and independence.

  • Health support

    Ongoing wellness support, including blood pressure monitoring and oxygen assistance.

  • Companion care

    Friendly companionship that encourages social interaction and community life.

  • Dining

    Chef-prepared meals served daily in a welcoming dining room.

  • Housekeeping

    Weekly housekeeping and personal laundry.

  • Activities

    Social activities, entertainment and celebrations that keep residents active and connected.

Questions families ask

Have a question that is not here? Call (830) 693-6446.

What types of apartments are available?

Studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments designed for comfort and accessibility.

Is assisted living available?

Yes. Residents can get help with daily activities such as bathing, dressing and medication management.

Are meals included?

Yes. Three chef-prepared meals are served daily.

Are activities provided?

Yes. The community offers weekly activities, social programs and live entertainment.

Can residents get help with medications?

Yes. Medication administration and health monitoring are available.

Are utilities included?

Yes. Utilities are included in the monthly cost.
