About Gemstone

Your loved ones, our top priority

A warm and welcoming memory care and assisted living community, where your loved ones experience exceptional care, personalized attention and a fulfilling lifestyle.

Our story

We are committed to raising the standard in senior living by redefining the assisted living experience through operational excellence, service-driven leadership and compassionate care.

Since 2014, we have worked to set a higher benchmark in senior care, one rooted in dignity, well-being and quality of life. We believe excellence begins with people, which is why we foster a culture centered on commitment, empathy and continuous professional growth.

As we grow, we stay focused on continuous improvement, service innovation and resident-centered care, creating places where people feel secure, supported and truly at home.

Our values

We create personalized care plans that address each resident's unique needs and preferences.

  • Resident-centered care

    Put the needs, wants and well-being of residents first, at all times.

  • Service excellence

    Commit to continuous improvement and the highest quality of service.

  • Compassion and empathy

    Act with kindness and understanding toward residents and their families.

  • A homelike, welcoming atmosphere

    Create a warm and secure space where residents feel comfortable and valued.

Our vision

To be the premier assisted living home in San Antonio, known for our outstanding care, our commitment to excellence and our warm and welcoming atmosphere.

We envision a future where our residents live their lives to the fullest, surrounded by love and support.

Our philosophy

To give families the highest level of service when they trust us with their loved ones.

At our communities, we are all about the wellness, comfort and safety of our beloved residents, guided by four core values: excellence, compassion, integrity and knowledge.
