Welcome to NorthStar

A closer look at NorthStar

Gemstone at NorthStar is a small, residential Type B certified memory care home in North Central San Antonio. We are set apart by a 27-year operational license and the longevity of our care staff.

Our dedicated 24/7 staff is trained to personalize care to each resident. Our home-like setting is comforting and embraces your family as our own. Feel right at home.

What do Type A and Type B mean?

Texas licenses assisted living communities by type. Type A is for residents who can leave the building on their own in an emergency and do not need routine care overnight. Type B is for residents who may need staff help in an emergency, or care during the night.

Good to know

Questions families ask

Have a question that is not here? Call (210) 941-5566.

When can residents have visitors?

NorthStar does not have restricted visiting hours. We just ask families to be considerate of other residents.

What activities are there for residents?

NorthStar has a wide variety of activities geared toward engagement and socialization.

What dementia-specific training does your staff receive?

NorthStar's staff has monthly training that includes dementia topics. Continuous training is imperative to the care of your loved one.

Do you require a background check for staff?

State regulations require a background check for all employees, along with a check of the employee misconduct registry.

Can I continue to use the same health care providers?

Yes, you can continue to take your loved one to the doctor of your choice.

Can staff administer medication?

Yes. Everyone who administers medication completes a medication class delegated by a registered nurse.

Do you have an in-house physician?

Yes, NorthStar offers in-house primary care.

Is your community licensed and inspected?

Yes, NorthStar is licensed and regulated by the State of Texas.

Do you allow hospice or end-of-life care?

Yes. Families can use the hospice of their choice when the time comes.

Do you offer transportation to doctor appointments?

At this time NorthStar does not offer transportation, but we can help coordinate wheelchair vans in the area, at the resident's expense.
